1. THE PROBLEM
--------------

Every day, Indian merchants using Razorpay lose a significant percentage of 
their revenue to failed or abandoned payments. The failure can happen for many 
reasons:

- Insufficient balance in the customer’s account
- Temporary bank / UPI downtime
- Network timeout between Razorpay and the bank
- Expired or revoked UPI Autopay / e-Mandate
- Customer abandons the checkout page
- Card expired or incorrect CVV
- Subscription charge fails on the due date

Most merchants currently handle these failures in a very crude way:
- Send a generic “Payment failed – please try again” email or SMS
- Retry the same payment blindly (which often fails again and annoys the customer)
- Or simply write the revenue off

This approach has three serious problems:
1. Low recovery rate (typically 15–25%)
2. No understanding of *why* the payment failed
3. No safety controls – an automated system can easily spam customers, 
   retry revoked mandates, or create infinite loops

As a result, money that could have been recovered simply disappears.

2. WHY THIS MATTERS RIGHT NOW
-----------------------------

- Digital payments in India are growing extremely fast.
- UPI Autopay and subscriptions are becoming common.
- Customers expect frictionless experiences.
- Merchants (especially SMEs) do not have large collections teams.
- AI can now diagnose the root cause and choose the right action, 
  but only if it is strictly bounded and auditable.

Razorpay’s own Track 3 brief captures this perfectly:
“Don’t just identify the problem. Show measured money recovered across a batch, 
with compliant escalation, stopping rules, and an audit trail.”

3. THE SOLUTION – Revyn
---------------------------

Revyn is a bounded, explainable AI agent that closes the loop from 
detection → diagnosis → decision → safe action → measurement.

Core Workflow
-------------

Step 1: Detect
- Ingest a batch of failed / at-risk payments (synthetic data + real Razorpay 
  Test Mode webhooks or payment objects).

Step 2: Diagnose (Root Cause)
- Rule-based engine first (fast & deterministic).
- LLM used only as a secondary classifier for ambiguous error messages.
- Output is a clean, typed root-cause label + confidence score.
  Examples: insufficient_balance, bank_timeout, mandate_expired, 
  network_error, customer_abandoned, card_expired, etc.

Step 3: Decide (Policy Engine – the most important part)
- A hard-coded, deterministic Policy Engine decides the action.
- The LLM is never allowed to execute money-moving actions directly.
- Policy rules include:
  • Maximum retry attempts (e.g. 3)
  • Economic floor (don’t recover very small amounts)
  • Time windows (e.g. retry insufficient_balance during salary days 1–5)
  • Hard stops for revoked / expired mandates
  • Escalation to human after certain failures
  • Kill-switch / global halt mode

Step 4: Execute (Bounded Actions only)
Allowed actions (strict allow-list):
- Create a new Razorpay Test Mode Payment Link / Order
- Schedule a smart retry
- Generate a personalized Hinglish WhatsApp / SMS recovery message
- Escalate to human review queue
- Mark as unrecoverable and stop

Step 5: Measure & Audit
- Every decision is written to an immutable audit log (timestamp, reason, 
  policy rule triggered, action taken).
- At the end of the batch the system reports:
  • Total revenue at risk
  • Revenue recovered (verified via Razorpay Test Mode)
  • Recovery rate vs a naïve baseline
  • Number of cases escalated / stopped
  • Full exception list

4. KEY DESIGN PRINCIPLES (Why this solution is strong)
-------------------------------------------------------

1. Safety First
   - LLM only diagnoses. All execution authority sits with a deterministic 
     Policy Engine.
   - Hard gates prevent dangerous behaviour (retrying revoked mandates, 
     infinite retries, etc.).

2. Measurable Impact
   - Judges can see exact rupees recovered on a held-out batch.
   - Comparison against a simple baseline (retry-everything-once) shows 
     real lift.

3. Full Audit Trail
   - Every action is explainable and logged.
   - Perfect for compliance and for the “show the audit trail” requirement.

4. Graceful Failure Handling
   - At least one realistic failure case is demonstrated 
     (e.g. Razorpay API down, webhook signature invalid, policy rejection).

5. Realistic Integration
   - Uses real Razorpay Test Mode APIs (keys starting with rzp_test_).
   - No real money is ever moved.

5. EXPECTED OUTCOME
-------------------

On a typical synthetic batch of 100–200 failed payments:
- Naïve retry baseline recovers ~20–25%
- Revyn recovers 35–45% (or higher) with far fewer customer annoyances
- 100% of decisions are logged and explainable
- Zero policy violations

This combination of real recovery numbers + strict safety + clear audit trail 
is exactly what the Track 3 bar asks for, and is strong enough to stand out 
in the shortlisting process for the AI Builder Internship.
